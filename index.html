Your privacy matters

Attendance Tracker is a student productivity application designed to help users manage attendance, subjects, timetables, notes, QR information, and college memories.

01

Information We Store

Attendance Tracker allows you to enter and store information within the App, including:

Student Profile Information

  • Full name
  • College/institution name
  • Branch/course
  • Semester
  • Roll number
  • Username
  • Password
  • Profile/avatar image
  • Target attendance percentage

Academic Information

  • Subject names and codes
  • Subject descriptions
  • Attendance records
  • Present/absent status
  • Attendance dates
  • Attendance notes
  • Timetable schedules
  • Classroom/room information
  • Academic categories or tags

Notes and Documents

  • Notes, titles and content
  • Categories
  • Images associated with notes
  • Locally generated PDF files

QR Code Information

  • QR code names and types
  • Descriptions
  • Links or QR data
  • QR images
  • Gate-pass information
  • Other information you choose to enter

College Memories

  • Memory titles and descriptions
  • Dates and tags
  • Photos/images
You control what you enter. Please do not enter sensitive personal information that is not necessary for using the App.
02

How Your Information Is Stored

Attendance Tracker is designed primarily as a local-storage application. The academic records, profile information, notes, QR information, timetable information, and memories created within the App are stored on your Android device using local application storage.

Images selected or created within the App may also be copied into the App's private internal storage.

We do not operate a server that stores your Attendance Tracker records as part of the current version of the App.

03

Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

In the current version of Attendance Tracker, we do not intentionally collect or request:

📍 Precise location information
👥 Contacts
📞 Phone call information
💬 SMS messages
🎙️ Microphone recordings
📷 Camera recordings
🪪 Advertising identifiers for personalized advertising
🔐 Biometric information

The App does not require these permissions for its core functionality.

04

Internet & Third-Party Services

The App may contain libraries and components that support Internet connectivity and Google/Firebase functionality.

However, in the current version, Attendance Tracker does not intentionally upload your attendance records, notes, timetable records, QR information, or college memories to a remote server operated by us.

If future versions introduce services such as cloud synchronization, analytics, authentication, advertising, AI services, or other third-party services that process personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

05

Photos & Images

Attendance Tracker allows you to select and save images for features such as:

  • Timetable photos
  • Notes
  • College memories
  • Profile/avatar images
  • QR-related images

Selected images are stored in the App's private storage on your device. The App does not intentionally upload these images to our servers in the current version.

You should only add images that you have permission to use and should avoid storing highly sensitive images.
06

Account & Password Information

Attendance Tracker provides a local account/profile mechanism. The username and password entered during setup are used by the App to provide local access/locking functionality.

These credentials are currently stored as part of the App's local data on your device and are not intended to be transmitted to our servers.

Security recommendation: Users should avoid reusing passwords that they use for important external accounts.
07

Data Deletion

You can remove information from the App using the deletion functionality provided by the App.

You may also remove the App and its associated application data through Android device settings.

Because the current version primarily stores information locally, deleting the App's data or uninstalling the App may permanently remove locally stored information. Before uninstalling the App, users should make any copies of information they wish to retain.

08

Data Security

We take reasonable measures within the App's design to keep locally stored information within the application's private storage.

However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Android device and for protecting access to your device and App.

09

Children's Privacy

Attendance Tracker is intended primarily for students and general users. The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children through a remote server.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us through a future online service associated with the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.
